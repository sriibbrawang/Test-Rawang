# Claude Idea Video

A saved video idea, studied from a TikTok by @akidahmad ("Ramai edit video guna Claude… Ni 5 step aku", 84 seconds, vertical 9:16).
Only the **idea and the motion style** are recorded here, not the content. Reuse it with our own footage and our own message.

---

## 1. The idea in one line

**One person talking to camera. Clean motion graphics pop up beside them to show each point, and a "step X / 5" progress bar shows how far along the video is.**

It feels like a friendly tutorial: the speaker explains, and the graphics show it.

## 2. Structure (timeline)

| Part | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0–4s | A big brand word fills the top of the frame (e.g. a logo and a single word), with small text above it ("ramai gila orang … edit pakai"). The speaker sits below it. |
| **Problem** | 4–7s | Short subtitle punch lines: "Tapi kau masih **lost**", "Hari ni aku bagitahu". A corner-bracket "camera frame" briefly appears around the shot. |
| **Promise** | 6–8s | One huge keyword drops in at the top ("install"), then a subtitle ("cara nak **install** dia"). |
| **Steps 1–5** | 8–67s | Each step has: a **"step N / 5" tag** top-left, a thin **progress line** across the top that grows each step, and **one UI card** that builds up as the speaker talks. |
| **Bonus** | 68–78s | The tag changes to "bonus · usage". A usage-limit card animates its bars, with a tilted sticky-note label ("2–3 video setiap 5 jam"). |
| **CTA** | 79–84s | The camera framing goes wider. A chat-bubble reply pops in ("Dah try, …"), then a card "Share link, biar aku tengok" with a red **"Share hasil kau"** button. |

## 3. Motion language (how things move)

- **Cards build piece by piece.** A card appears empty, then each line or row is added as the speaker says it: the price row, then the second price row; folder 1, then folder 2, then folder 3; a prompt that "types" itself line by line.
- **Highlight follows the voice.** In a list, the row being talked about turns blue, with its note on the right ("video raw", "contoh + b-roll", "video siap").
- **Pop and settle.** Elements scale in quickly with a small overshoot, sit still while they're discussed, then fade or slide out. Nothing floats around for no reason.
- **One focus at a time.** Only one graphic is on screen at any moment, usually in the lower half so the face stays clear.
- **Keyword emphasis in subtitles.** Subtitles are white bold sans, and **one keyword per line is coloured blue** ("lost", "install", "time", "prompt", "panda").
- **Mixed type for flavour.** The occasional word is set in an *italic serif* in blue ("lawa-lawa", "Pro", "edit") for a playful contrast with the bold sans.
- **Sticky-note labels.** Short facts sit on a white, slightly rotated label with a soft shadow ("Code = pandai *edit*", "Siap dalam 20–30 minit").
- **Big word drops.** For the most important word, huge black bold text fills the top third for about one second.
- **Small camera moves.** There are jump cuts between sentences, with slight punch-in zooms to keep the energy up.
- **Progress bar.** A thin blue line at the very top grows with each step, so viewers know how much is left.

## 4. Visual style

- **Background:** a real, warm, softly lit room (cream and beige). The graphics are dark.
- **Cards:** dark charcoal (#1f1f1f–#2a2a2a), rounded corners (~16px), thin border, soft shadow, mac-style window dots on the "folder" card.
- **Accent:** one blue (selection rows, keywords, progress line) plus a single red for the final CTA button.
- **Type:** bold geometric sans for subtitles and UI, a monospace tag for "step 1 / 5", and an italic serif for accent words.
- **Safe zones:** nothing in the bottom ~18% or the right edge, where TikTok puts its buttons and caption.

## 5. Reusable template (fill in for any topic)

1. **Hook (0–4s):** big keyword plus a small setup line. *"Ramai orang ___, tapi ___."*
2. **Problem and promise (4–8s):** two punchy subtitles, then one big keyword drop.
3. **Steps (5 × ~10s):** "step N / 5" tag, progress bar, one building card per step.
4. **Bonus tip (~10s):** one useful extra fact on a sticky note or card.
5. **CTA (~5s):** "Share / comment / save" card with a bright button.

Total: 60–85 seconds. Speak in short sentences, with a cut between each one.

## 6. How we can use it at SRIIBB

- **"5 things we learned at Teachers Training Day 1":** a facilitator or teacher talks to camera. Cards show each session: Assembly → Reset your intention → Ice breaking → Islamic assessment → DOPE test. The DOPE step can build up the four birds one by one (Dove, Owl, Peacock, Eagle).
- **"Which DOPE bird are you?":** step through the four birds, highlighting each one's strength. The CTA: "Comment your bird 👇".
- **Parent explainer videos:** e.g. "5 steps to register at SRIIBB", with building cards for documents, fees and dates.

**What we need to film:** one person, a quiet warm-lit spot, a clip-on or handheld mic, and phone footage in 9:16. Record short sentences, one point at a time.

## 7. Production notes

- Edit with Claude Code plus HyperFrames or Remotion for the motion graphics, and FFmpeg for the cuts. Transcribe the speech first so the graphics can be timed to the words.
- Keep a `projects / reference / end project` folder setup: raw footage, reference videos and b-roll, finished videos.
- Add the music or trending sound inside TikTok when you post.
