# Kinetic Caption Video

A saved video idea, studied from a TikTok by @theresalearns about "Claude Council" (79 seconds, vertical 9:16).
Only the **idea and the motion style** are recorded here. Reuse it with our own footage and message.

---

## 1. The idea in one line

**One person talks to camera. Their words appear word by word as stacked captions, where small white words sit around one big serif keyword in yellow. Screenshots and evidence slide into the top half of the screen while they talk.**

## 2. Structure

| Part | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Hook (0–4s)** | A brand card pops up at the top while the first keyword ("Claude") lands big in yellow serif. |
| **Problem (4–20s)** | Screenshots of articles and posts slide into the top half. A yellow highlighter sweeps across the key sentence. The speaker stays visible in the lower half. |
| **Turn (20–40s)** | Headline graphics ("Claude is the world's best yes-man.") fill the top. The captions keep stacking below. |
| **Solution (40–65s)** | A big claim graphic ("5 advisors. 5 reviewers. 1 verdict.") with the product name dropped on top in serif. Then a UI screenshot of how to use it. |
| **Close (65–79s)** | Logo stickers pop in at the frame corners. The final keyword lands big ("TIPS", "objektif"). |

## 3. Motion language

- **Word-by-word captions.** One to three small white bold sans words appear in sync with speech, stacked in 2–3 lines.
- **One hero word per phrase.** The important word is much bigger, set in a **yellow condensed serif** (like Instrument Serif), and sits on its own line under the small words. Examples: "*Claude*", "*tanya*", "*benar*", "*kesimpulan*".
- **Captions sit low-centre,** over the speaker's chest, never covering the face.
- **Evidence slides into the top half.** Screenshots, articles and slides come in from the top or side as a card covering about the top 40%. The speaker continues below.
- **Highlighter sweep.** A yellow marker swipes left to right across the key line of a screenshot as it's mentioned.
- **Sticker pops.** Logos scale in with a little bounce at a corner, then stay.
- **Moody base.** Dark background and warm light, so the white and yellow type stands out.
- **Fast cuts.** A new caption every 0.3–0.8s keeps the eye busy, while the shot itself barely changes.

## 4. Visual style

- **Type:** a bold sans for the small words, and a tall condensed serif in **yellow (#F5E04A-ish)** for hero words.
- **Shadows:** a soft dark shadow under all captions. No boxes behind small words.
- **Screenshots:** shown as real crops (dark or light), sometimes slightly tilted, filling the full width.

## 5. How we adapt it at SRIIBB

- **Readable without hiding the video.** Keep the small words small. When a line must be big, put it on a **frosted glass panel**: the video behind is blurred and darkened slightly but still visible.
- **Hero keyword in yellow serif** for names and key ideas, e.g. "*Ulul Albab*", "*MRSM Bagan Datuk*", "*hospitality*".
- **Slide screenshots slide into the top half** with a highlighter sweep on the key line. This works well for presentations we attend.
- **Combine it with the Claude Idea Video style** (`ideas/claude-idea-video.md`) when a step-by-step structure helps.
