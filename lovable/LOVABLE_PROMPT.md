Title: One Team, One Mission: SRIIBB Teachers Training, Day 1
Date: Monday, 16 November 2026
Tags: SRIIBB, Teachers Training, Reflection
Cover image: [close.jpg]

Intro:
Our first day brought teachers from different campuses into one room. We gathered for assembly, reset our intention, broke the ice, strengthened our Islamic foundation and discovered our personalities through the DOPE test. Here is how the day went and what we are taking with us.

## 1. Assembly · 8:40 AM
[fit1.jpg] [fit2.jpg] [fit3.jpg]
What we did: The day opened with all trainees standing together for assembly, setting the tone for the week ahead before the first session began.
Our reflection: Starting together matters. Before we are teachers of a subject or a class, we are one SRIIBB family with one mission: kejayaan dunia akhirat for our pupils.

## 2. Reset your intention · 9:40 AM
[psy1.jpg] [psy2.jpg] [psy3.jpg]
What we did: En. Hilmi led a session that asked us to pause and look inward: why we chose to teach, and why we chose SRIIBB.
Our reflection: Teaching is an amanah. When our niat is right, every lesson, every duty and every tired moment becomes ibadah. A clear intention is what keeps a teacher going on the hard days.

## 3. Ice breaking · 12:25 PM
[plan1.jpg] [plan2.jpg] [plan3.jpg] [plan4.jpg]
What we did: Aunty Fatonah got us moving with "Beyond the Name Tag" and "Find Your People", where we hunted for colleagues who matched each prompt. We mixed with teachers from other campuses and each wrote a commitment for our first day at SRIIBB.
Our reflection: "We made you into peoples and tribes so that you may know one another" (Al-Hujurat 49:13). Strangers in the morning became teammates by noon. Different campuses, one mission.

## 4. Islamic assessment · 2:30 PM
[ust1.jpg] [ust2.jpg]
What we did: After lunch, every teacher sat for the Islamic assessment.
Our reflection: At an integrated Islamic school, every teacher is a role model of deen, whatever subject we teach. The assessment helped us see our own foundation honestly, so we know what to strengthen before we guide the children.

## 5. DOPE personality test · 4:15 PM
[eng1.jpg] [eng2.jpg] [eng3.jpg] [eng4.jpg] [eng5.jpg] [eng6.jpg]
Show a small row of 4 cards:
- Dove: The Supporter (the heart)
- Owl: The Thinker (the mind)
- Peacock: The Communicator (the spirit)
- Eagle: The Leader (the engine)
What we did: Aunty Ainin took us through the DOPE test. We scanned a QR code, answered honestly and found our bird. Then we worked in groups to "Build Your Dream SRIIBB Team" on chart paper and presented it.
Our reflection: Understanding ourselves helps us understand others. A school needs all four birds. The colleague who works differently from us is not difficult. They bring a strength we do not have.
What have I learned about myself today? What is one communication habit I can improve tomorrow?

## Overall reflection: Different personalities. Different strengths. One SRIIBB team.
[close.jpg]
Day 1 was about people before programmes. We aligned our intention, got to know one another, checked our foundation and learned how each of us works best. We go home tired but connected, ready for Day 2.

Takeaways:
- Renew our niat every morning: teaching is an amanah.
- Greet colleagues from other campuses by name.
- Keep the commitment we wrote on our first day.
- Keep strengthening our own Islamic foundation.
- Work with every bird: Dove, Owl, Peacock and Eagle.
